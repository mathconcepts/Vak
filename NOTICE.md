## Timing data

The timing data in `packs/` is derived by measurement from the texts and recordings above. It contains word and line times, not audio.

Sri Rudram (Namakam, Taittirīya Saṁhitā 4.5, and Chamakam, 4.7): text with Vedic svara marks from the sanskritdocuments.org editions (doc_veda/rudra and doc_veda/chamaka), encoded and proofread by Rajagopal Iyer and Rekha Venkatesh. Words and swara marks are copied unchanged; only line breaks were chosen for reading. The recordings used for timing are not included in this repository.

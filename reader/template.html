Created by MSP Giri, inspired by Navarathri 2026. With Jagadamba's blessings, more will follow.

Chant with the recording

  • Press Music at the top, then the big Play button.
  • The word in gold is the word being sung. The page moves by itself.
  • Tap any line to jump there. Drag the slider to move through the recording.
  • Faded lines are not sung in this recording.

Swaras and pauses (Vedic texts)

  • Blue, line under a syllable: anudātta, sung low. Orange, line over: svarita, the rise. Orange, double line: dīrgha svarita, the long rise.
  • While the recording plays, the zoom lens above the line shows the whole line, large, as a melody: svarita syllables sit higher, anudātta syllables lower, and a soft ribbon runs through them. The ribbon fills with gold as the line is sung, the syllable being sung lights up, and the next swara syllable glows just before it comes. Its swara is named below. Word timings come from the recording; within a word, syllable timing is estimated from short and long syllables.
  • ’ marks a pause the reciter takes in this recording. It glows while the pause is happening.
  • Tap any word to hear it from the recording.
  • Practise: More → Practise a portion. Tap the first word, then the last word (or tap a line, or choose the whole anuvāka). It plays that portion again and again. Choose how many times, a pause after each time to repeat it yourself, and a slower speed. The pitch stays true when slowed.
  • The zoom lens and focus line can be switched off in More. To keep attention on one place, turn on Nearby lines only. Show progress tells you which anuvāka and line you are on.

Chant on your own

  • Press Recite, then Listen. Stay quiet for one second.
  • Chant at your usual pace. The light follows you and waits when you pause.
  • If it runs ahead or behind, press Back or Next once. It learns your pace.

Little helpers

  • The screen stays on while the app is open. To let it sleep: More → Keep screen on.
  • Aa makes the letters bigger or smaller.
  • More → Auto-scroll off lets you scroll the page yourself.
  • More also has the speed, your own recording, and timing fixes.
  • Bluetooth speaker running late? Aa, then Calibrate by tapping.
  • Tap the prayer name at the top left to open the library or add a new prayer.
